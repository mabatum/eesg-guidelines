## Clinically meaningful source differences retained

- AP 90/120 every 4 weeks or 75/100 every 3 weeks; 4 preoperative cycles.
- Source describes additional AP after >90% necrosis, HD I / young-patient HD MTX after <90%.
- Ewing source calls the doxorubicin-containing block VAC and alternates with IE every 3 weeks.
- Bone-marrow biopsy is recommended for Ewing in this source.
- Denosumab is recommended neoadjuvantly for operable GCTB; source days 1/8/15/28 preserved.
- Stable long-term GCTB: source maintenance every 3 months; progression: return to every 28 days.
- Follow-up every 3 months for 2 years, then every 6 months to year 5.
- Chordoma imatinib 400 mg/day; conventional CS dasatinib or pazopanib.

## Source ambiguities retained explicitly rather than silently normalised

- Table 1 uses <8 / >8 cm, leaving exactly 8 cm undefined. The site flags the gap.
- Bone resection margin is ≥5 cm in the recommendation and 3–5 cm in the comment.
  The site does not repeat the claim that a margin guarantees no recurrence.
- Necrosis ≥90% in explanatory text versus >90% in the treatment branch.
- Four AP cycles before and after surgery would total 600–720 mg/m² doxorubicin.
  The site explicitly requires cumulative-dose/cardiotoxicity assessment.
- Poor-response adjuvant switch differs from the prior evidence-review edition;
  the source recommendation is attributed and the discrepancy is visible.
- GemTax interval is absent in the relevant source paragraphs: no interval is invented.
- Different introductory/specific Ewing RT doses are distinguished.
- Calcium/vitamin D do not replace dental prevention of jaw osteonecrosis.
- Chordoma comment names sorafenib, but main regimens name sunitinib; no sorafenib dose is invented.
- Administrative legal citations and service-code catalogues remain in the full PDF;
  they are not represented as independently verified current law.
